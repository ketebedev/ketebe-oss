# Code of Conduct

Ketebe aims to maintain a technical community where people can collaborate respectfully and evaluate ideas on their merits.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate professionally and respectfully,
- discuss technical decisions without attacking people,
- give actionable feedback and assume good faith where reasonable,
- respect different levels of experience and ask for clarification constructively,
- keep security reports and private project information in the appropriate private channels,
- respect contributor attribution and licensing obligations.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in Ketebe project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation or discriminatory abuse,
- personal insults or sustained hostile behavior,
- publishing another person's private information without permission,
- deliberately disrupting technical discussions or contribution workflows,
- knowingly disclosing embargoed vulnerabilities, secrets or private commercial source,
- repeated bad-faith behavior after maintainer guidance.

## Scope

This policy applies to Ketebe repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project-controlled community spaces. It also applies when someone is representing the project in an official capacity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject or remove contributions and comments that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately when appropriate, and focused on protecting productive participation rather than winning disagreements.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not escalate sensitive conduct reports through public issue threads. Contact project maintainers privately with enough context to investigate the concern while minimizing unnecessary personal information.
